# Pay Equity Methodology — Reference

Used by `equity-gate`. A practical method for testing whether pay is fair — i.e., explained by
role, skills and impact, and free of protected-attribute proxies. Not a legal certification.

## The core test

> For comparable work, is every pay gap **explained** by role, skills or impact?
> If a gap is **unexplained**, it is a finding — and a VETO until remediated.

## Method

1. **Define comparables.** Group by **level + core skills** (the job architecture), not title.
   Comparing unlike roles either hides real gaps or invents false ones.
2. **Measure variance.** Within a comparable set, look at the spread of pay (and compa-ratios).
3. **Attribute the variance.** For each gap, ask: is it explained by proficiency, scope, impact or
   location? Document the explanation. What you can't explain is the finding.
4. **Audit the inputs.** Check how placements were decided. Flag any protected attribute or **proxy**:
   - **Prior salary** (imports historical, often gendered/racialized, bias)
   - **Negotiation strength** (correlates with demographics, not value)
   - **Legacy title / tenure** (stands in for, but isn't, skill)
5. **Verdict.** Issue **PASS** or **VETO**. For VETO, list findings + remediation owners and re-run.

## Protected attributes (never inputs)

Sex/gender, race/ethnicity, age, disability, religion, national origin, pregnancy/family status,
and any proxy that correlates with them. Pay maps to the **work**, not the **person**.

## Remediation patterns

- Re-place an under-paid profile on level + skills (owner: comp-strategist).
- Remove prior-salary anchoring from the placement logic.
- Re-level a role whose definition was driving the gap (owner: role-designer).
- Escalate to legal/compliance where pay-equity law applies.

## Boundaries

- This is a **safeguard**, not legal certification — jurisdictions with pay-equity law need formal review.
- It audits **structure**, not individuals' worth; it never sets an individual's pay.
- **No time-pressure waivers** — "fix it later" on pay equity becomes legal and trust debt.

## Anti-patterns

- Pooling unlike roles into one "equity" comparison.
- Accepting "that's just what they negotiated" as an explanation.
- Treating prior salary as a neutral, fair anchor.
- Waiving a finding to hit a comp-cycle deadline.
