# Pay Equity & Fairness Gate — Checklist

Owner: **equity-gate** (Tier 3). **Mandatory** before any band or leveling is published or communicated. Has **veto power**. No time-pressure waivers.

## A. Comparable set (blocking)
- [ ] The comparable population is defined by **level + core skills**, not title alone.
- [ ] Like is compared with like; unlike roles are not pooled into one comparison.

## B. Explained variance (blocking)
- [ ] Every pay gap between comparable roles is **explained by role, skills or impact**.
- [ ] Any **unexplained** gap is documented with a remediation owner (→ VETO until closed).

## C. No protected-attribute proxies (blocking)
- [ ] No protected attribute (or proxy) is an input to pay/leveling logic.
- [ ] **Prior salary** is not used as an anchor (it imports historical bias).
- [ ] **Legacy title / tenure** is not standing in for demonstrated skill.
- [ ] **Negotiation strength** has not driven placement.

## D. Compression & structure (blocking)
- [ ] Band overlap across levels checked; no unmanaged compression (new hires near tenured peers).
- [ ] Leveling is consistent with the skills-based job architecture (role-designer).

## E. Legal & accountability (blocking)
- [ ] Jurisdiction-specific pay law identified; **legal/compliance certification** required where it applies.
- [ ] Output is framed as **advisory** — individual pay decisions stay with accountable humans.

## Verdict
- [ ] **PASS** — all blocking items satisfied → bands/leveling may be published.
- [ ] **VETO** — list findings, assign remediation owners, require re-run.

> A band you cannot explain by role, skills and impact isn't a band — it's a liability.
