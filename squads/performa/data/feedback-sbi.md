# Feedback: SBI (Situation — Behavior — Impact)

Good feedback describes **what happened and its effect**, not the person's character. SBI keeps it specific, fair and actionable — and avoids the labels that calibration-gate flags as bias.

## The model
- **Situation** — when/where it happened (context).
- **Behavior** — the specific, observable action (what they did/said).
- **Impact** — the effect on the work, team or outcome.

## Examples

❌ Label: *"You were unprofessional in the meeting."*
✅ SBI: *"In yesterday's planning meeting (S), you interrupted two colleagues mid-sentence (B), and they stopped contributing for the rest of the call (I)."*

❌ Label: *"Great job, you're a rockstar."*
✅ SBI: *"On the outage Friday night (S), you isolated the root cause in 20 min and wrote the prevention task (B), which cut our MTTR and stopped a repeat (I)."*

## Why it matters here
- Feeds **evidence-based reviews** — SBI notes are exactly the examples a rating needs.
- Keeps 1:1s concrete and balanced (reserve space for the person first).
- Removes coded/character language ("aggressive", "not a culture fit") that the calibration gate vetoes.

## Tips
- Separate behavior from interpretation; describe, don't diagnose.
- Tie growth feedback to a next step (links to the PDI).
- Reinforce strengths with SBI too — not just corrections.
