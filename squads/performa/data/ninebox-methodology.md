# 9-Box Methodology (performance × potential)

The 9-box positions a person on **two independent axes**, each justified by its own evidence. Performance is *what they delivered*; potential is *their capacity to grow into bigger/different scope*. Never derive one from the other, and never use personality as the potential axis.

## The grid

| | Low potential | Medium potential | High potential |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High performance** | Trusted Professional | High Impact | Star |
| **Medium performance** | Effective | Core Player | Growth |
| **Low performance** | Underperformer | Inconsistent | Enigma / Diamond-in-rough |

## Performance axis (evidence)
From the review: delivery, quality, collaboration, ownership against the role's competencies and objectives. Recent + full-period, anchored examples.

## Potential axis (distinct signals)
- **Learning agility** — speed of mastering the unfamiliar.
- **Scope growth** — handling more ambiguity/complexity/scale over time.
- **Aspiration** — desire and readiness for broader responsibility.
- ❌ **Not** personality, likeability, tenure, or "potential vibe".

## Using the cell
Each cell implies a **development move**, not a verdict:
- **Star / High Impact:** stretch, sponsor, retain.
- **Core Player:** deepen mastery; targeted potential signals.
- **Growth / Enigma:** clarify expectations; experiential stretch in ambiguity.
- **Underperformer / Inconsistent:** evidence-based improvement plan.

## Limits
- A 9-box is **input to a human conversation**, not a promotion/pay decision.
- Placements with thin evidence on either axis are **low confidence** → calibrate.
