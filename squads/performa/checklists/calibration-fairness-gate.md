# Calibration & Fairness Gate — Checklist

Owner: **calibration-gate** (Tier 3). **Mandatory** before any rating or 9-box placement is shared. Has **veto power**. No time-pressure waivers.

## A. Evidence sufficiency (blocking)
- [ ] Every competency rating cites a recent, concrete example.
- [ ] No "empty 3s" — missing evidence is marked *insufficient evidence*, not a default.
- [ ] 9-box: both axes (performance AND potential) have their own evidence.

## B. Rater calibration (blocking)
- [ ] Each rater's distribution compared to the cohort; leniency/severity outliers normalized with evidence.
- [ ] 360 disagreements reconciled with evidence, not averaged away.

## C. Bias / disparate impact (blocking)
- [ ] Ratings/9-box audited for demographic skew across the group.
- [ ] No coded/character language ("aggressive", "not a culture fit", "rockstar") — feedback uses SBI.
- [ ] Recency and halo effects checked and addressed.

## D. Personality weight-zero (blocking)
- [ ] Personality/style (profiler-dna) contributed **0** to any rating or 9-box axis.
- [ ] Behavioral context appears only in development sections (PDI/1:1), clearly labeled weight 0.

## E. Decision framing (blocking)
- [ ] Promotion/pay implications are framed as **evidence-based input for the accountable human**, not automatic outputs.

## Verdict
- [ ] **PASS** — all blocking items satisfied → results may be shared.
- [ ] **VETO** — list findings, assign remediation owners, require re-run.

> Rate the work, calibrate the raters. A number you can't explain from evidence isn't a rating — it's a guess.
