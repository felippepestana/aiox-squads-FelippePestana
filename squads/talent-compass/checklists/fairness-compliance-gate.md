# Fairness & Compliance Gate — Checklist

Owner: **fairness-gate** (Tier 3). This gate is **mandatory** and has **veto power**. No recommendation, comparison, or report leaves the squad until every blocking item passes. There are no time-pressure waivers.

## A. Language audit (blocking)
- [ ] No coded/biased language in notes or report ("culture fit", "not polished", "too aggressive", "not a team player" without evidence).
- [ ] Every evaluative statement is tied to a concrete, role-relevant example.
- [ ] No comparisons to an "ideal type" of person rather than to the performance objectives.

## B. Consistency audit (blocking)
- [ ] The same interview guide was used for every candidate for this role.
- [ ] The same BARS anchors and rubric were applied to each candidate.
- [ ] No candidate received materially more/less probing without justification recorded.

## C. Personality weight-zero (blocking)
- [ ] DISC contributed **0** points to the score.
- [ ] Enneagram contributed **0** points and was used only for development/onboarding, with consent.
- [ ] The report states explicitly that behavioral style is context, not a decision factor.

## D. Protected-attribute leakage (blocking)
- [ ] No question or note references age, race, religion, marital/family status, pregnancy, health/disability, or national origin.
- [ ] Screening decisions are justified by objective-fit evidence, never by a protected attribute.
- [ ] No proxy variables standing in for protected attributes (e.g., "culture", neighborhood, school as a class signal).

## E. Decision framing (blocking)
- [ ] Output is framed as a **recommendation with evidence and confidence**, not an automatic accept/reject.
- [ ] The accountable human is named as the decision-maker.

## Verdict
- [ ] **PASS** — all blocking items satisfied → release the recommendation.
- [ ] **VETO** — one or more blocking items failed → list findings, assign remediation owners, and require `*recheck` after fixes.

> Reminder: a clean process is the defense. If you cannot show *why* a score was given from evidence, the score is not valid.
