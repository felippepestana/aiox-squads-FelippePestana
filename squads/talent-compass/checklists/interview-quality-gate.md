# Interview Quality Gate — Checklist

Owner: **guide-builder** + **evidence-scorer**. Ensures each interview produces scorable, comparable evidence before scoring proceeds.

## A. Guide quality (before the interview)
- [ ] Every competency maps to at least one question.
- [ ] Every scored question has BARS anchors (1 / 3 / 5 defined).
- [ ] Behavioral questions are designed to elicit STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
- [ ] Probing follow-ups are written to dig for the candidate's specific Action and Result.
- [ ] One guide per role; no per-candidate variation of core questions.

## B. Evidence capture (during/after the interview)
- [ ] Each core question was asked (none skipped).
- [ ] Responses captured as evidence, not as scores or opinions.
- [ ] For each behavioral question, the STAR elements are present or explicitly noted as missing.
- [ ] Where an answer stayed abstract, a probe was issued for the specific Action.

## C. Scorability (before scoring)
- [ ] Each competency has at least one concrete example to rate against its anchor.
- [ ] Gaps and low-confidence areas are flagged for follow-up rather than guessed.
- [ ] Behavioral style notes (if any) are kept separate and labeled weight 0.

## Verdict
- [ ] **READY** — evidence is sufficient and comparable → proceed to `score-candidate`.
- [ ] **INCOMPLETE** — re-ask or schedule a focused follow-up before scoring.
