# STAR + BARS Methodology

The two methods that make talent-compass interviews evidence-based and fair. STAR structures the *answer*; BARS structures the *rating*. Together they turn an interview into a defensible prediction.

## STAR — eliciting evidence

For behavioral questions, push every answer to cover:

- **S — Situation:** the context the candidate faced.
- **T — Task:** what they were responsible for.
- **A — Action:** what **they specifically** did (the most important part).
- **R — Result:** the measurable outcome and what they learned.

Common failure mode: candidates speak in "we" and stay abstract. The interviewer's job is to **probe for the Action**: "What did *you* personally do?", "How did you decide?", "What was the measurable result?"

## BARS — Behaviorally Anchored Rating Scales

For each scored question, define what a 1, 3 and 5 answer *looks like in behavior* before the interview. This keeps ratings consistent across interviewers and candidates.

Example (competency: problem-solving under pressure):

| Rating | Behavioral anchor |
|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | Froze or guessed; no structured approach; blamed circumstances. |
| 3 | Broke the problem down; reached a workable solution; partial result. |
| 5 | Isolated root cause systematically; weighed trade-offs; measurable fix; prevented recurrence. |

Scores 2 and 4 are "between" anchors. Always cite the response that justifies the rating.

## Why this matters (validity)

Structured interviews with behavioral anchors are among the most predictive selection methods (Schmidt & Hunter; Google re:Work). Unstructured "gut feel" interviews are weakly predictive and a common entry point for bias. The combination of:

1. **Performance objectives** (what success means),
2. **Structured questions** (same for every candidate),
3. **BARS anchors** (consistent rating),
4. **STAR evidence** (specific, scorable),
5. **A fairness gate** (bias/compliance veto),

is what lets talent-compass defend every number on the scorecard — and keep personality at weight 0.

## Interviewer reminders
- Same core questions for every candidate; adapt only probes.
- Capture evidence during the interview; score afterward.
- No score without a cited example.
- "Culture fit" is not evidence — a specific, role-relevant behavior is.
