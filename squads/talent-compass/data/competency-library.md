# Competency Library (role-agnostic)

A reusable starting set of competencies with sample BARS anchors. `role-architect` derives the actual competencies from a role's performance objectives — this library is raw material, not a fixed template. Adapt names and anchors to the role; never force-fit.

Each competency lists sample anchors for a 1 (weak), 3 (solid) and 5 (strong) demonstration. Anchors must be made concrete to the role during `define-role`.

## Behavioral competencies

### Ownership & accountability
- 1: Waits to be told; deflects responsibility when things go wrong.
- 3: Owns assigned work; raises blockers; follows through.
- 5: Takes end-to-end ownership beyond their lane; drives outcomes and post-mortems without prompting.

### Problem-solving under pressure
- 1: Freezes or guesses; no structured approach.
- 3: Breaks the problem down; reaches a workable solution.
- 5: Isolates root cause systematically; weighs trade-offs; prevents recurrence.

### Collaboration & influence
- 1: Works in isolation; friction with others; no give-and-take.
- 3: Works well across a team; communicates clearly.
- 5: Aligns stakeholders with competing interests; elevates the team's output.

### Communication
- 1: Unclear, rambling, or one-directional.
- 3: Clear and structured; adapts to the audience.
- 5: Makes complex topics simple; drives shared understanding and decisions.

### Learning agility
- 1: Repeats mistakes; resists new approaches.
- 3: Learns from feedback; picks up new tools.
- 5: Rapidly masters unfamiliar domains; teaches others.

## Technical / functional competencies (examples — replace per role)

### Domain expertise (role-specific)
- 1: Surface knowledge; relies on others for fundamentals.
- 3: Solid working knowledge; handles standard cases independently.
- 5: Deep expertise; handles edge cases; sets the standard for others.

### Execution quality
- 1: Frequent rework; misses requirements.
- 3: Reliable, meets requirements with normal review.
- 5: High-quality, low-defect output; raises the quality bar.

### Analytical / data
- 1: Decisions by intuition; ignores data.
- 3: Uses data to inform decisions.
- 5: Builds the analysis others rely on; quantifies trade-offs.

## Motivation signals (align to the work, not to "passion" theater)
- Intrinsic interest in the actual day-to-day of the role.
- Energy for the role's hardest, least glamorous parts.
- A track record of seeking the kind of problems this role contains.

> Reminder: competencies must predict the role's performance objectives. If a competency does not map to an objective, drop it.
