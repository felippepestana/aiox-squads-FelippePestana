# Inclusion, Consent & Accessibility Gate — Checklist

Owner: **inclusion-gate** (Tier 3). **Mandatory** before an onboarding journey runs. Has **veto power**. No time-pressure waivers.

## A. Consent & data ethics (blocking)
- [ ] Informed consent recorded for any behavioral/personal data used.
- [ ] Behavioral context (profiler-dna) is framed as **weight 0** (tailors pace/communication, never judges).
- [ ] Only data needed for onboarding is used; not repurposed without new consent.

## B. Accessibility (blocking)
- [ ] The new hire was invited (safely, with consent) to share accommodation needs.
- [ ] Materials available in accessible formats (screen-reader, captions, alt text).
- [ ] Assistive tech / scheduling accommodations are in place for day 1.

## C. Inclusion (blocking)
- [ ] No exclusionary language; acronyms/culture explained for newcomers.
- [ ] No protected-attribute misuse or proxies in the journey or tracking.
- [ ] Deliberate introductions/buddy to prevent isolation (esp. remote/hybrid).

## D. Augment-the-manager (blocking)
- [ ] The buddy/IA supports — does not replace — manager 1:1s, direction and belonging.
- [ ] Risk flags are framed as prompts for support, never verdicts.

## Verdict
- [ ] **PASS** — all blocking items satisfied → the journey may run.
- [ ] **VETO** — list findings, assign remediation owners, require re-run.

> An onboarding that excludes — or that judges people by traits — isn't onboarding.
