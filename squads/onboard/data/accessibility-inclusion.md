# Accessibility & Inclusion in Onboarding

Onboarding must work for **everyone**. The `inclusion-gate` audits these before any journey runs; the other agents design with them in mind from the start.

## Accessibility
- **Ask, with consent**, about accommodations (don't assume); make it safe to disclose.
- Provide materials in accessible formats (screen-reader friendly docs, captions on videos, alt text).
- Ensure tools/assistive tech work on day 1 (test, don't hope).
- Be flexible on scheduling (time zones, focus time, medical/assistive needs).

## Inclusion & belonging
- Use inclusive, jargon-light language; explain acronyms and culture for newcomers.
- Pair the hire with a buddy; make introductions deliberate, not left to chance.
- Watch for isolation (especially remote/hybrid) and act early.

## Fairness guardrails
- **No protected-attribute misuse / proxies** in journeys or tracking (age, gender, race, religion, disability, etc.).
- **Behavioral context (profiler-dna) is weight 0** — it tailors pace/communication, it never judges or gates the person.
- **Risk flags are prompts for support**, never verdicts.
- The buddy/IA **augments** the manager relationship; belonging and 1:1s stay human.

## Data ethics (LGPD-aware)
- Informed consent for any behavioral/personal data; collect the minimum; treat as sensitive.
- Use data only for its stated onboarding purpose; never repurpose without new consent.

> An onboarding that excludes — or that judges people by traits — isn't onboarding. The gate has veto power.
