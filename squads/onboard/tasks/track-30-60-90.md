# track-30-60-90

## Task: Track 30/60/90 Milestones & Surface Risk

### Metadata
- **executor:** milestone-tracker
- **elicit:** false
- **mode:** analytical
- **output:** milestone-status.yaml

### Inputs Required
```text
journey-30-60-90.yaml
signals: progress, check-ins done, engagement signals
```

### Elicitation
```text
(uses the journey + current signals)
```

### Execution Steps

#### Step 1: Compare to journey
- Check progress against the 30/60/90 plan and due check-ins.

#### Step 2: Surface early risk
- Flag slipping items and disengagement signals early, with context.

#### Step 3: Prompt the human
- Suggest a manager 1:1 / next step; frame risk as support, not a verdict.

#### Step 4: Summarize ramp
- Give a concise status against the journey for the manager.

### Output Format
```yaml
milestone_status:
  new_hire: {name}
  day: {n}
  on_track: [{milestone}]
  slipping: [{milestone, why}]
  risk_signals: [{signal}]
  suggested_next_step: {manager 1:1 / unblock ...}
  framing: support, not a verdict
```

### Veto Conditions
- Cannot treat a risk flag as a verdict on the person
- Cannot replace manager check-ins
- Cannot use personality as a risk factor

### Completion Criteria
- Progress compared to the journey
- Slipping items + risk signals surfaced early
- Human next step suggested (support framing)
- Concise ramp status produced
