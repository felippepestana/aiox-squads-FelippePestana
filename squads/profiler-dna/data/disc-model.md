# DISC Model (work-style context — weight 0)

DISC describes **tendencies in observable work style**, used here only as context for communication, management and onboarding. Never a selection/promotion/pay factor.

## The four dimensions

| Dim | Drive | Tends to look like | Works best when |
|-----|-------|--------------------|-----------------|
| **D — Dominance** | Results, control | Direct, fast, decisive | Autonomy, clear goals, bottom-line first |
| **I — Influence** | People, recognition | Enthusiastic, persuasive, relational | Interaction, visibility, room to ideate |
| **S — Steadiness** | Stability, harmony | Patient, consistent, supportive | Stability, clear expectations, time to adapt |
| **C — Conscientiousness** | Accuracy, quality | Analytical, precise, careful | Data, standards, time to do it right |

Most people are a **blend** (e.g., high-D/C, high-I/S). Always describe the blend, not a single letter.

## Reading guidance
- Infer from concrete language and examples, not vibe.
- Report **confidence** (low/medium/high); a few sentences = low confidence.
- Translate to action: how to communicate, delegate and give feedback.

## Limits
- Limited predictive validity for job performance — do not present as predictive.
- A self-report or short text is a snapshot, not a fixed identity.

> Every DISC output carries: *"Work style is context for how to work with this person — weight 0 in any people decision."*
