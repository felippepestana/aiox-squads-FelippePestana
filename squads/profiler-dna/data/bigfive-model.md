# Big Five / OCEAN Model (trait context — weight 0)

The Five-Factor Model is the most empirically validated trait framework. Used here as **context** with weight 0 — never a selection/promotion/pay factor.

## The five traits (as tendencies)

| Trait | High tendency | Low tendency |
|-------|---------------|--------------|
| **O — Openness** | Curious, imaginative, variety-seeking | Practical, conventional, routine-preferring |
| **C — Conscientiousness** | Organized, dependable, goal-driven | Flexible, spontaneous, less structured |
| **E — Extraversion** | Outgoing, energized by people | Reserved, energized by focus/solitude |
| **A — Agreeableness** | Cooperative, trusting, warm | Direct, skeptical, competitive |
| **N — Neuroticism** | Sensitive to stress signals, reactive | Calm, even, stress-resilient |

## Reading guidance
- Each trait is a **continuum**, not a binary; report where the evidence points and the confidence.
- Cite the specific behavior/answer behind each read.
- Describe Neuroticism **neutrally** (e.g., "attuned to risk") — never pathologize.

## Limits
- Traits are tendencies that vary by context and shift over time.
- A short sample yields low confidence — say so.

> Every Big Five output carries: *"Traits are tendencies, not destiny — weight 0 in any people decision."*
